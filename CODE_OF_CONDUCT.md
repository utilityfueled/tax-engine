# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want this project to be useful, rigorous, and respectful.

Participants are expected to:

- Be direct without being abusive
- Assume good faith while reviewing technical work critically
- Keep discussions focused on facts, tradeoffs, and reproducible behavior
- Respect privacy and avoid posting sensitive data

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, insults, or intimidation
- Doxxing or sharing private information
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Deliberate disruption of community spaces

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, lock threads, or block participation for conduct that harms the project or its contributors.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately rather than escalating publicly.

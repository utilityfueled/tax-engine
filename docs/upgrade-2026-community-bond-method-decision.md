# Community bond partition: interpretation still required

Reviewed October 28, 2026. This is a federal savings-bond source-method
investigation, not a selection or qualification of California return support.
The current noncommunity reissue calculations remain available within their
documented scope. Changed community ownership remains guarded.

The unresolved case is an actual California community EE/I bond held by the
same two living spouses. A signed property agreement and Treasury reissue would
give each spouse a separate successor containing exactly half the principal,
face amount and event-date value. There is no payment using other assets, later
ownership change or commingling. Actual purchase payments, domicile, marriage,
property terms, original registration and each owner's previously reported
interest must remain source records. A payment made by one spouse must not be
rewritten as two fictional payments.

[Publication 550, Community property and Purchased jointly](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550)
describes equal community interest and separate reissues preserving original
purchase proportions. Its ordinary spouse-transfer paragraph expressly excludes
community bonds. Applying the proportional-reissue example to this exact
community-to-separate partition is an inference; the located text does not
expressly decide it. The missing matching example is not itself a prohibition.

[Revenue Ruling 2002-22, Effect on Other Documents, printed page 852](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-irbs/irb02-19.pdf)
removes the assignment-of-income references in Revenue Ruling 87-112 while
reaffirming its section 454 and transferee-basis treatment. Section 1041 or
unchanged aggregate wealth alone therefore does not establish that this event
recognizes no deferred interest. [26 CFR 1.454-1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.454-1)
was also checked; this review has not established its application to the exact
partition from the located text.

[Publication 555](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p555),
[2025 FTB Publication 1031](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-1031-publication.pdf)
and [California BOE letter 2018/014](https://boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/lta18014.pdf)
support collecting actual property character, domicile and written conversion
terms. The BOE letter is historical agency corroboration, not a federal bond
ruling. Current California legislative and court full-text endpoints returned
HTTP 403; historical statutory material was not substituted for verified
current operative law.

A qualified reviewer must establish whether this exact equal partition is a
section 454 disposition, its deferred-interest recognition and successor basis,
and the operative state-law conditions. The resulting source contract must
distinguish actual physical payments from any derived beneficial ownership.
The ordinary noncommunity helper cannot admit this case by removing its guard.
Reduced education exclusions attributable to two educators remain a separate
interpretation question even if the ownership question is resolved.

The proposed arithmetic is conditional, not an approved tax result. For original
principal 500, event value 700 and two equal successors each later redeemed for
500, a zero-recognition treatment would retain principal 250 per successor.
Actual prior reported interest 20 per owner and later reported interest 10/5
would then leave 220/225 at redemption. No zero-recognition result has been
implemented or marked reviewed merely because these amounts reconcile.

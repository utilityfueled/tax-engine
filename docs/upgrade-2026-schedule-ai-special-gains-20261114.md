# Schedule AI special-rate original worksheets

November 14, 2026. This package adds actual cumulative period worksheets for
collectibles and unrecaptured section 1250 gain to the existing annualized
Form 2210 method. It does not qualify final 2026 forms or filing.

The original signed sum of 28% worksheet lines 1–6 and section 1250 worksheet
line 13 are collected separately for all four periods. Negative signed 28%
amounts offset section 1250 gain once. Each full-year component must match the
completed original Schedule D worksheets; an unchanged combined total cannot
hide a reclassification. Blank amounts remain unknown, zero remains zero, and
edits revoke confirmations. Physical review generation requires the current
original input and recomputed result before accessing the template.

Authority: [final 2025 Form 2210 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i2210),
[Schedule D instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040sd), and
[2026 Publication 505](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505). Four official
bodies were retrieved and hashed. The existing section 1202, investment-interest
election, Schedule J, Form 8615 and Form 2555 method gates remain in effect.

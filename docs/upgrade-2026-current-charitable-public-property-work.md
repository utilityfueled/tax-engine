# Current public cash and ordinary property

This bounded extension calculates both current-year carry candidates from actual public cash and ordinary public property, with an identity-bound, confirmed empty prior history. Ordinary property uses the smaller of actual fair market value and actual basis. Unknown basis, unknown holding character, private gifts, capital-gain property, source totals that differ from the actual receipts and NOL sources remain explicit guards. The existing collector supplies the original sources; no new default or inferred history is introduced.

Sources refreshed October 25, 2026: [IRC 170(b)(1)(A), (G), (I), (d)(1)(C), (e)(1)(A), and (p)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t26/s170/) and [Reg. 1.170A-10(a)(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.170A-10). The 2026 reciprocal current G/A limits resolve as `G=min(current cash,60% base)` and `A=min(ordinary property,max(0,50% base-G))`: when G is at least 50% of base, A is zero; otherwise A cannot exceed 50% of base less G, so the 60%-less-A cash limit is also satisfied. This establishes the current-only result without inventing an ordering election. The itemized floor applies to A before G. Nonitemization retains only each category's percentage excess, independently of the current section 170(p) deduction. The numerical controls and non-standard-entitled nonitemizer extension are documented statutory derivations, not published IRS examples or human tax qualification.

Independent literal controls:

- Base 100,000, current cash 40,000 and ordinary property 30,000: before-floor G/A are 40,000/10,000; itemized deduction is 49,500. Itemized property carry is 20,500; nonitemized property carry is 20,000.
- Current cash 70,000 and property 30,000 at that base: before-floor G/A are 60,000/0; itemized cash deduction is 59,500. Itemized carries are cash 10,500 and property 30,000; nonitemized carries are 10,000 and 30,000.
- Current cash 10,000 and property 20,000 at that base have no percentage excess. The floor reduces the property deduction, but creates no carry.
- At base 10,000.49, cash 4,000.11 and property FMV 5,000.49/basis 3,000.24, exact A capacity is 1,000.135 and the floor is 50.00245. Itemized property carry is 2,050.10745; nonitemized property carry is 2,000.105. The original FMV and basis are retained.
- A known zero basis on a positive-FMV ordinary property gift produces zero value; it is not treated as an unknown source. Actual zero contribution base retains all current percentage excess.

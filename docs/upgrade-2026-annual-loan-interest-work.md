- [IRC 529(c)(3)(B)(ii), (v), (vi), and (c)(9)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t26/s529/): the existing annual cash fraction follows actual expense qualification, source reductions and the actual borrower lifetime limit.
- [IRC 221(a), (b), (d), and (e)(1)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t26/s221/): qualified loan interest is reduced by the relevant tax-free QTP distribution earnings before the deduction cap and phaseout.
- [Publication 970, chapters 4 and 7](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p970): loan interest cannot receive both benefits; the annual QTP worksheet uses the year's actual gross distributions and earnings.

This is a determined bounded cohort: one actual interest-only loan-payment receipt is the sole positive qualified expense of a complete ordinary no-ESA cash pool for one year, beneficiary and recipient. Its actual receipt reductions are zero, and no prepaid distribution supplies a competing qualified benefit. Explicit unrelated or zero-cost records remain source facts. Removing this interest receipt leaves zero qualified costs. Therefore the statutory counterfactual and the publication's interest-use description both identify the same excluded earnings, `sum(actual positive Box 2) * min(actual interest, remaining lifetime capacity, annual gross) / annual gross`. No cash-account expense split, payment priority or user-entered exclusion is inferred. Original receipt allocations remain unchanged.

The existing independent lender/origination/owner/qualification sources still determine the Section 221 deduction. The calculated annual ratio may establish the linked QTP reduction without requiring a user to enter a computed or repeating decimal. A preserved explicitly supplied reduction must reconcile; omission without the determined calculated context remains unknown. Exact rational amounts carry through the deduction cap and phaseout before reported-dollar rounding, without changing global Decimal precision.

Derived controls are fictional original-source examples, not published IRS examples: annual gross 30,000, earnings 9,000 and interest 1,000 produce excluded 300 and taxable 8,700; actual eligible lender interest then gives a 700 deduction. Interest 8,000 with actual prior 3,000 and other current use 2,000 gives qualified 5,000, excluded 1,500 and net lender interest 6,500 before the 2,500 deduction cap. A repeating two-thirds net-interest amount at a three-quarters remaining phaseout yields exactly one-half, reported as one dollar.

Remaining methods stay explicit: mixed principal/interest or other positive qualified purposes require a separately established attribution; mixed pre/post-July-4 sources retain their source-date method; ESA allocations, prepaid competition, other-year refunds and bond reissues are not inferred here. Existing legacy, unknown-versus-zero, review-only PDF, final-year and independent human qualification gates remain unchanged.

# Ohio 2026 documentary timely balance payment

The ordinary employee draft accepts a complete original-bound balance receipt
when the reported balance and the balance using exact original withholding are
both positive and at most $500. The second limit is a conservative admission
policy, not a professional interpretation of statutory rounding. Full-year,
part-year and nonresident employee fixtures exercise the same source method.

The receipt must identify the actual taxpayer account, tax year, IT 1040 payment,
date, amount and confirmation, with complete confirmed inventory. It must be paid
January 1 through April 15, 2027, no later than the actual as-of date, and match
the fresh reported balance exactly. Expected, partial, excess, future or stale
receipts and unsupported prior/estimated/extension/other-balance facts deny.

The reported return `amountDue` remains income tax minus withholding. A separate
projection records documentary cash applied and its resulting outstanding
reported balance. It does not claim an independently observed account settlement
or classify the receipt as withholding, estimated tax or an income deduction.
Private names, SSNs, account references and confirmations stay out of results
and audit entries. Existing withholding-only and historical methods retain their
behavior; reciprocal, Virginia-credit and business drafts gain no payment method.

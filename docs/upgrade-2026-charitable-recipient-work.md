Ordinary and crypto sources, current-gift validation, Schedule A, the public-only carry and NOL methods, guided entry and the original review binding now use the same source distinction.

A qualifying operating foundation, or another foundation within subsection (F), belongs to the 50%-limit recipient category. A private nonoperating foundation outside (F) uses the other-recipient percentage category and the applicable capital-property basis reduction. A different qualified 30%-limit organization uses that percentage category without acquiring the private-foundation valuation rule. These source categories do not resolve unrelated special-property deductions or open an unsupported carry method.

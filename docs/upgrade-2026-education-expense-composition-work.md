This bounded package gives each actual postsecondary tuition, required books/equipment, eligible computer/internet and connected special-needs receipt its own qualification and source-reduction boundary. It composes the existing prepaid/cash QTP and actual ESA allocation methods without requiring a fabricated prepaid benefit on every receipt. Original distribution amounts, benefit costs, identities, annual cash ratios and account basis remain independently reconciled.

The source adds actual dated receipt-linked refund records and non-tuition AOTC/LLC expense character. The new refund cohort is same-year cash received and retained by the beneficiary, with a complete confirmed ledger; a different recipient, historical refund, recontribution or corrected benefit remains a precise separate method. No default zero, qualification, allocation, refund chronology, tax credit or account basis is supplied. Edits revoke root and record confirmations and remain locally saved. Review context binds original source records before redaction; no filing or year-readiness gate changes.

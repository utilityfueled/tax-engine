# Actual payer amounts and determined own-return advance repayment

This private package composes actual owner payments that differ from allocated Part IV premiums with the completed zero-credit, fully reported repayment method. The original policy, independently allocated premiums and advances, each person's cash and advances, monthly qualification and business limit remain separate source facts. It adds no tax amount, repayment allocation or election to the input schema.

## Authority and bounded derivation

Primary sources refreshed on 2026-10-27 are [Regulation 1.162(l)-1(a)(1)–(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.162(l)-1), [Revenue Procedure 2014-41, sections 3–4](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2014-33_IRB), [final Form 7206 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i7206), [Publication 974](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p974), and the actual Part IV/Part V rules in [Form 8962 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8962). This is a derived sufficient composition, not a published combined numerical example or independent professional qualification.

For a completed actual return with zero credit and full repayment of the advances attributable to this return, every such exact advance is restored when exact source advances equal the reported repayment. Each person's actual cash plus that person's own-return advance remains independently bounded by the allocated specified or nonspecified premium component. Form 7206 applies actual relationship, payer, month, establishment and business-income limits. An original policy shared with another return can have twice the advances; that other return's advances or repayment are never imported.

When exact source advances differ from reported Form 8962 amounts, the admitted source is one fully eligible owner/business cost pool. Its actual paid cost is the lesser of the original eligible cost and actual owner cash plus the reported repayment. Exact unpaid source cents and any reporting excess remain visible. A reporting increase cannot create premium expense; a multiple-owner or partly ineligible pool cannot invent an allocation of a reporting difference.

Marriage-year composition uses the existing complete actual date, family, person, payment and monthly original-source binder. The final alternative calculation is independently recomputed with its actual income and dates. It must retain zero credit and full own-return reported repayment after the proposed health deduction. A source-only intake result is not proof that a lower-income return will meet this condition.

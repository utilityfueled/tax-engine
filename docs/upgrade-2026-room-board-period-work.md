This private package retains actual institutional periods and receipt membership, applies one applicable allowance or larger actual institutional housing invoice, and carries the result through the canonical calculation, guided interview and review PDF. It adds no entered exclusion or taxable-income override. Receipt, prepaid benefit, account basis and original distribution amounts remain unchanged. A source edit revokes the annual, period and receipt confirmations; actual zero, false and missing answers remain distinct.

Official authorities refreshed October 26, 2026: [IRC 529](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t26/s529/), [IRC 530](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t26/s530/), [Publication 970](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p970), and [Notice 2016-13](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2016-07_IRB#NOT-2016-13). Section 529(e)(3)(B) supplies the academic-period/living-arrangement cap and half-time requirement. Section 529(c)(3)(B)(i)/(ii) supplies the prepaid-before-cash sequence; subsection (vi) and section 530(d)(2)(C) supply ESA coordination. The allocation records for that ESA cohort are actual source choices, reconciled to calculated period capacity. Notice 2016-13's repeal of QTP account aggregation does not establish an allocation election between competing QTP originals.

The finite supported cohort has periods wholly within the return year, uniquely linked actual paid receipts, known zero education-credit/other-benefit use for room-and-board costs, and consistent beneficiary/recipient identities. Distinct nonoverlapping periods have separately sourced caps, with no time proration. One original distribution, a determined single prepaid/single cash sequence, nonbinding multiple originals and an actual ESA coordination choice are supported, including composition with the separately validated tuition/assistance method. The annual Coverdell worksheet retains actual account basis and its existing common ratio. False enrollment or institution qualification yields no exclusion; it is not changed to an affirmative answer.

Exact remaining boundaries are explicit: multiple prepaid originals competing for a binding cap without ESA; multiple cash originals competing for a reduced residual; periods spanning tax years without separately represented prior-use history; and incompatible recipient or overlapping period records. The first two require an established controlling allocation method, which the refreshed primary sources did not establish.

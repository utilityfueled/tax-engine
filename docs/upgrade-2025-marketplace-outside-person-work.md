# Actual cash paid for an under-27 child outside the tax family

This private package adds a source-complete nonspecified-premium method for actual owner/business cash paid for an under-27 child who is covered by an original shared Marketplace policy but belongs to another tax family. It preserves the original policy allocation, family benchmarks, receipts, other-person cash and independent final credits. It does not provide an entered deduction, payment percentage or override of this return's credit. Source/software review and filing qualification are separate.

Primary sources refreshed on 2026-10-26:

- [IRC 162(l)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapB-partVI-sec162.htm), [Form 7206 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i7206) and [Publication 974 Worksheet P](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p974) include otherwise qualifying amounts paid for the owner's child under age 27, even when not a dependent, subject to actual plan, employer-month and business-income requirements.
- [Rev. Proc. 2014-41 sections 3-4 and Example 3](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2014-33_IRB) treat otherwise deductible outside-tax-family premiums as nonspecified before the specified-premium calculation. Example 3 uses a non-Exchange child policy. Applying section 4 to this source-complete shared-QHP cohort is a derivation, not a published numerical example.
- [IRC 280C(g)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapB-partIX-sec280C.htm) coordinates a same-premium deduction with the credit. [Reg. 1.36B-3(c)(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.36B-3) permits another person's payment to support a claimant's credit. Zero advance credit therefore does not establish the other claimant's final credit. This package admits an independently completed other-return Form 8962 with actual line 24 zero, year/claimant/person/policy links, reference and complete-policy evidence. This is a sufficient source cohort, not a statement that the IRS universally requires obtaining another person's return. Positive cross-return credit and repayment attribution remain a separate method question; the 2014 temporary-regulation preamble also describes the limitation in terms of the taxpayer's credit.
- [Reg. 1.162(l)-1(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.162(l)-1) and Publication 974 keep the specified premium and attributable credit/advance limitations separate from the independently qualified nonspecified cash.

## Source and calculation boundary

The original shared allocator continues first. Complete person identity links distinguish this-return family members from the outside child. The outside original premium pool is the full original premium less this return's independently allocated family premium. Actual outside cash and advances cannot borrow from that family pool. Original receipts must still conserve full premium, owner/business cash, other cash and advances, and every covered person's actual payment attribution remains explicit.

For an eligible under-27 outside child, original outside advances must be zero. Every positive actual child payment requires the independent completed other-return record. Unknown, draft, missing, duplicated, conflicting-year/claimant/policy and positive-credit records cannot become an inferred zero. The schema permits blank facts during intake; the calculation does not infer their values. Source edits revoke the existing health, family and Marketplace confirmations. Copying original policies does not invent another return.

Qualified child cash enters ordinary nonspecified Form 7206 once. It never enters this return's coverage-family SLCSP or specified premium. Actual employer-month and payer qualification and the owner's existing business-income limit still apply. Known age 27 or a known ineligible relationship preserves the original cash but allows no child deduction. A source-only intake check does not establish this return's final credit; existing completed family-calculation methods remain controlling.

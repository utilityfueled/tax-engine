# Shared policies before marriage and health worksheets

This package composes the existing actual Part IV policy allocation, alternative marriage calculation and business health worksheets. It retains each full original Form 1095-A and independently dated/person-linked receipts. No allocation fraction is treated as evidence of who paid a premium. No calculated credit, income or deduction override is introduced.

## Source derivation

Official [2025 Form 8962 instructions, line 9 and Table 3](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8962) require Part IV before Part V when both apply. [Publication 974, Table A tip and Steps 2/4](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p974) exclude other-taxpayer allocated amounts before the alternative worksheets. Original policies sent to a filer follow that recipient's Step 2(a)/4(a) group; originals sent to neither filer use the actual alternative-family membership in Step 2(b)/4(b).

The existing global answer that no marriage or divorce occurred cannot be true in the year of the filers' marriage. The new per-policy source answer and reference instead establish that the other taxpayer was not married to either filer during the year. This supports the existing Situation 3/4 classification without inferring a divorce or a separate-return allocation. The pure allocator can run before the independent marriage worksheet is complete; the canonical calculation still requires all marriage evidence.

For a policy sent to neither filer, this package admits months whose actual this-return people belong to one alternative family. A policy spanning both alternative families, or overlapping a filer statement for the same own person, retains an explicit within-return premium-grouping boundary. It does not split an original premium equally or manufacture separate original amounts. Outside people remain in full originals, assignments and receipts, and are excluded from this return's alternative benchmark membership.

The existing health source binder first reconciles all original covered people, own/other cash, actual advances and allocated premiums. Marriage health checks then preserve actual own-person qualification before the wedding. Positive owner-paid outside-person marriage qualification remains a separate method. The applicable authorities for actual payment and health limitations remain [26 CFR 1.162(l)-1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.162(l)-1), [26 CFR 1.36B-4](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.36B-4), Publication 974 and the independently implemented Form 7206 source methods.

## Bounded remaining health methods

This package does not claim all Marketplace combinations are complete. The established finite queue still distinguishes:

- Determined ordinary and shared-policy marriage grouping and actual own payments: this package, independently reviewed within the stated software/source scope and awaiting final integration.
- Actual partial coverage-family and premarriage dependent/child qualification in marriage years: reuse the applicable existing original person/month facts where the authorities determine the result; do not infer qualification from an end-year relationship. Positive outside-person marriage payments are not enabled here.
- Positive cross-return credit and positive noncoverage advances with partial/capped repayment: preserve actual policy/payment/other-return evidence and the precise attribution question. A zero-other-credit source cohort is sufficient for the delivered outside-person method, not a universal legal condition.
- Binding 2025 joint capped repayment across two owners: downstream QBI proportions are not authority for the upstream owner repayment split. The unresolved owner attribution remains explicit.
- A policy sent to neither filer with both alternative families, or a same-person filer/other-recipient overlap: requires its determined within-return premium grouping; this package does not invent it.

All unblocked work stays within the execution brief's finite payer/person/marriage scope. No new health regime, broad eligibility election or external qualification is claimed.

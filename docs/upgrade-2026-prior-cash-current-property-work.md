# Prior public cash and current ordinary property

This bounded 2026 method retains actual prior public cash and current ordinary public property as separate categories. It supports a positive contribution base only when the allowed current property absorbs the entire itemized floor. It also supports a zero contribution base, where the floor is zero. It does not assign any floor to current or prior cash. Original contribution years, source history, cents, fifth-year use and expiration remain intact.

Sources refreshed October 25, 2026: [IRC 170(b)(1)(G), (I), (d)(1)(C), (e)(1)(A), and (p)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t26/s170/), [Reg. 1.170A-10](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.170A-10), and [Pub. 526 carryovers](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p526). Current cash uses its G allowance before actual prior G in FIFO order. Current A is limited by 50% of the contribution base less aggregate allowed G, which also satisfies the reciprocal 60%-less-A ceiling. The floor reaches A before G. This branch requires current A to absorb that entire floor. Current percentage excess permits the eligible current A floor amount to carry with its current origin; otherwise the floor creates no carry. Nonitemization retains percentage excess and original surviving prior balances independently of the current section 170(p) deduction. These are bounded statutory derivations, not published IRS numerical examples or human tax qualification.

The source collector and facts-only validator are reused unchanged. Actual prior records must be public cash with complete identity and history. Every current ordinary property receipt retains its actual FMV and basis and contributes the smaller amount. Unknown basis or holding character, private or capital-gain sources, other prior categories, inconsistent totals, and NOL sources remain explicit guards. Final deduction selection chooses between the internally calculated itemized and nonitemized carry candidates; the caller cannot enter a calculated carry or infer standard-deduction entitlement.

Literal controls:

- Base 100,000, current cash 10,000, prior cash 20,000 and ordinary property 30,000: before-floor G/A are 30,000/20,000. Current cash deduction is 10,000, current property deduction 19,500 and prior cash deduction 20,000. Current A carry is 10,500 if itemized, or 10,000 if nonitemized.
- Base 100,000, no current cash, prior cash 49,500 and property 5,000: allowed A exactly equals the 500 floor. Current property deduction is zero; its carry is 5,000 itemized or 4,500 nonitemized. Changing prior cash to 49,500.01 rejects because .01 of the floor would reach the unresolved cash attribution method.
- At that base, prior cash 1 and current property 500 have no percentage excess. The full property allowance is lost to the floor and no floor-only carry is created.
- At zero base, actual prior 2020 cash .03 and 2021 cash .04 expire; 2025 cash .05 survives. Current cash .01 and property .02 retain their current year. Reordering records changes no result.
- The canonical base-10,000 control reports Schedule A current cash 1,000, current property 1,950, prior use 2,000 and total 4,950. Automatic standard and explicit nonitemization retain A carry 1,000; itemization retains 1,050. Explicit nonitemization with actual standard ineligibility keeps the standard deduction at zero. Prior treated-paid use remains 2,000 while its actual deduction is zero outside itemization.

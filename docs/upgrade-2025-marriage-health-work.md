# Marriage-year Marketplace and self-employed health composition

This package composes the existing actual alternative-marriage calculation with the existing self-employed health worksheets. Independent agent source and saved-original/PDF reviews are clear within the stated scope. The reviewed rebase and local integration checks are complete; this is not independent human tax or form qualification. It accepts independently complete, unshared original policies and full coverage-family health plans. It preserves actual joint income, alternative family assignments, dates, original payments, owner business limits and the existing allocation method. It adds no entered deduction, income, credit, repayment or allocation percentage.

## Sources and calculation

Primary sources refreshed October 26, 2026:

- [Reg. 1.36B-4(a)(3)(iii) and (b)(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.36B-4) distinguish the self-employed repayment limit from the alternative pre-marriage calculation. The latter can reduce excess advances; it cannot generate additional net credit.
- [Publication 974](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p974), Worksheet X and Simplified Steps 2–4, uses the completed interim credit and then refigures the final credit. Its multiple-business instructions expressly allow a reasonable allocation of the monthly credit, including enrollment premiums. The ordinary existing monthly enrollment allocation remains in force; this package does not impose a new recipient-local health allocation.
- [Form 8962 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8962), Table 2, line 28 and Part V, supply the applicable contribution rates, self-employed worksheet direction and actual marriage boundary. A wedding on the first day of February through December stops alternative months before that month; January 1 is ineligible.
- [Reg. 1.162(l)-1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.162(l)-1) and [Form 7206 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i7206) retain actual qualified people, payment, premium, credit and business-income limits.

The source-only marriage binder may recognize an explicit health-coordination answer. The new marriage-health binder then requires the actual confirmed health packet and matches every covered health person to original policy identities and coverage months. Equal amounts cannot cure a different person. An end-year spouse label cannot establish qualification for a pre-wedding payment and coverage period. Other covered people require the existing original identity links; the sufficient premarital dependent/child cohort also has actual separate-owner qualification evidence. This is a source applicability boundary, not a statement that every under-27 child must be a tax dependent.

The canonical interim and final calculations carry the already computed historical X/Y/Z limit. The new reconciliation checks the completed marriage family/month result against actual sources, actual modified AGI, the internal ordered stage and that computed cap. Positive alternative monthly credit can coexist with actual joint income ineligibility only when the source calculation establishes it. Actual joint eligibility is never relabeled.

Original all-zero months outside coverage remain source records. The health source and guide recognize the explicitly zero original and reconciliation; unknown or positive amounts are not discarded. The existing PDF mapper already supports that known-zero month, so its added output control requires no mapper change.

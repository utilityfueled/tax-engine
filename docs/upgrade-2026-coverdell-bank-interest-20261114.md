# Actual bank interest after a Coverdell cash conversion

This bounded federal source method admits an actual January 1 whole-account
Coverdell disqualification followed that day by a custodian-documented cash
conversion to the primary beneficiary's own domestic bank deposit. The actual
original Form 1099-INT remains Schedule B income once; it is not added again to
the deemed distribution or education additional-tax base. A continued trust,
other owner, foreign deposit, other activity or incomplete original remains
unresolved and rejected.

The source packet requires actual ownership, domestic classification, custodian
conversion, complete annual cash activity, exact opening-plus-interest equals
closing cash and known zero for other flows and income. The whole original INT
and its snapshot must match. Optional original year or owner metadata cannot
disguise a duplicate bank account statement. Independent actual accounts remain
separate. No source document or income is synthesized.

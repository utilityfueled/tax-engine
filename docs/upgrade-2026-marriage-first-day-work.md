The source derivation is recorded in `spec/federal/form-8962-marriage-first-day.yaml`. Refreshed October26,2026: [Reg1.36B-4(b)(2)(ii)(B)-(C)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.36B-4) distinguishes partially unmarried months from fully married months. [Final2025 i8962 Table4](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8962) requires January1 unmarried status and coverage before the first full married month. The generic [Pub974 TableA](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p974) wedding-month shorthand does not discuss day1. The implementation follows the regulation's specific distinction, preserving Pub974's ordinary separate-family formulas.

For a February through December day1 wedding, the alternative stops in the preceding month. Later-day weddings retain that month. January1 is ineligible and now has an accurate explanation. The original date and wedding month are retained; a separate required internal `lastPreMarriageMonth` carries the derived cutoff. It is not accepted as a taxpayer override. The source binder, guided copying, worksheet comparison, monthly transfer and PartV all use the same boundary. Stale alternative benchmarks for the fully married month reject rather than disappearing silently.

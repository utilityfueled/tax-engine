# Pennsylvania employee reimbursements included in taxable wages

Pennsylvania Schedule UE line I excludes allowances the employer already included
in PA-taxable W-2 compensation. Actual qualifying expenses enter line H and flow
through line J. Schedule NRH line 19 is for reimbursements outside W-2 wages.
The state's final 2025 instructions establish the form treatment. Pennsylvania's
ongoing expense guidance supports the preliminary 2026 source method. The
federal source for a fully taxable reimbursement is the employer's original
payroll and Form W-2. [PA Schedule UE](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/revenue/documents/formsandpublications/formsforindividuals/pit/documents/2025/2025_pa-40ue.pdf),
[PA Schedule NRH](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/revenue/documents/formsandpublications/formsforindividuals/pit/documents/pa-40nrh.pdf),
[PA expense guidance](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/revenue/resources/tax-types-and-information/personal-income-tax/unreimbursed-business-expenses),
[IRS W-2 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw2w3).

The finite typed cohort requires one signed employer proof, all actual
compensable work performed in Pennsylvania, equal original Box 1 and PA Box 16
wages, and same-date payroll reconciliation from cash wages before each allowance.
Each receipt and allowance is bound to its original once. With $30,000 in cash
wages, a $3,500 allowance fully included in both boxes and $3,000 in eligible
receipts, the result is $33,500 in gross compensation, $3,000 on UE H and J, zero
on UE I and NRH 19, and $30,500 net PA compensation. The actual eligible receipts
also apply when the fully included allowance is smaller. The method handles
supported resident, Pennsylvania-service part-year and nonresident returns.

inputs before composition. The existing Ohio S corporation exception remained
valid in the distinct replay. Partial or split taxable inclusion, excess cash
outside Box 16, mixed physical work and taxable allowance intake in the guided
interview still require source methods. The review and filing gates remain closed.

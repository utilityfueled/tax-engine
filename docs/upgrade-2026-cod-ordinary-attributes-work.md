# Ordinary cancellation-of-debt attribute sources

This private package replaces the ordinary one-event insolvency aggregate shortcut with actual source records for the later section 108 attributes. It follows the existing fully excluded unsecured personal-debt method and preserves the current-tax NOL, modified-absorption and charitable-comparison guards. Main, rule metadata, inventory and implementation links are parent-owned. Source base: `238535feeb6cd648f087d27f2c68e790faa33006`.

## Source contract

The original debt, immediately-before insolvency worksheet and independent regular/AMT NOL histories remain required. Complete actual later ledgers retain original years, original-year closing amounts, every intervening filed use/reduction/expiry, actual current use/carryback and both property inventories. Missing facts are not zero. Current-origin amounts have an explicit distinct meaning. Original expired balances cannot be laundered through current use.

The section 108 sequence is NOL, general business credit, minimum tax credit, capital loss, basis, passive losses/credits, then foreign credits. Credit attributes are reduced by one third of the excluded income applied. Exact rational components retain that distinction. The immediately-after basis/money/liability snapshot supplies the section 1017 cap; the next January 1 inventory supplies the property actually reduced. Property priority and proportional basis allocation follow Regulation 1.1017-1. A post-discharge sale is not admitted to the canonical return without its actual current transaction/recapture reconciliation.

Shared general business, minimum-tax and passive credits are recorded once. Positive reductions compose only when both independently sourced relevant regular/alternative financial vectors and actual NOL reductions agree. This is a sufficient equal-vector composition, not an allocation rule between differing chains. Both remaining COD amounts being zero permits unchanged credits. Exact competing same-priority allocations remain guarded.

Beginning-next-year minimum tax credit is not inferred from current Form 8801. The initial package proves a zero signed current source adjustment only with actual AMT zero, AMT foreign credit zero, final ATNOLD zero, no deferral in any individual original or netted other item, no Form2555, equivalent capital-gain facts and complete current-source confirmation. A nonzero signed current-source adjustment is a finite internal follow-through using the separately reviewed source-year primitive; that primitive and its future integration are not included here.

A transferred final-year estate/trust capital carry cannot be relabeled as a newly incurred current loss to bypass the original-year/current-use contract. Regulation 1.642(h)-1(b) preserves the carry; this method requires the missing transferred history rather than inventing it. Zero carries do not add a restriction.

## Official authority and source meaning

- [Treas. Reg. 1.904-2](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol11/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol11-sec1-904-2.pdf), paragraphs (b), (c)(3)(ii), and (d): the actual deduction election does not prevent deemed absorption of prior unused credit. The counterfactual uses the credit-election limitation; it creates no actual current credit. Prior category/year sources retain chronological application and their actual expiration.
- [IRC59](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-136/t26/s59/), (a)(1) and (3), and [IRS6251 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i6251), line8 Steps1–6: AMT uses its separate amounts and tax system. The simplified limitation numerator is available only from the actual qualifying first-claim election history. Separate adjusted AMT carry records cannot be copied from regular credit records.
- [IRC56](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-136/t26/s56/), (b)(1)(A), preserves the actual AMT treatment of taxes and standard/itemized deductions.
- [IRS1116 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1116), line10, corroborate the deduction-year hypothetical method. [IRS982 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i982), PartII, put the current-tax determination before attribute reduction; the existing one-for-three credit reduction and Form982 aggregate dollar boundary remain unchanged.

The independently confirmed empty regular and AMT original NOL lists remove only a presence-based guard. They do not stand in for missing histories or positive original losses that later expired or were exhausted. The completed current return must also have no positive newly generated Form172/461 loss. Foreign/domestic loss-account sources and coupled positive-loss methods remain separate.

For deduction years, the canonical private counterfactual supplies actual computed regular income/tax and AMTI/pre-credit tax. It does not invent next-year inputs, a current credit, an election, or an entered tax-limit scalar. A confirmed empty independent AMT inventory needs no invented election history. A positive inventory requires actual first-claim/election facts and confirmed source amounts, and derives its own limitation and absorption.

Each original record conserves opening = actual current use + actual carryback + derived deemed absorption + before-COD balance. Normal expiration remains separate; after-COD remaining plus after-COD expiry equals the balance left after reduction. Positive derived absorption requires one determined original record per category/year; competing original allocation remains guarded. Source-only validation may defer only the affected final-year expiry equality until the computed counterfactual is available. No completed return or output may defer it.

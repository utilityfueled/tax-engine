# Remaining education method decisions

Source review: 2026-10-25. These boundaries do not qualify a tax year, filing
artifact or human review. Missing source facts must be supplied from actual
records; an approval cannot substitute for a tax calculation method.

| Method | Current evidence and remaining action |
| --- | --- |
| Joint community-property bond income | **Implemented and verified in this package.** Pub550 assigns half of community bond interest to each spouse. The separate bounded branch requires actual community status throughout accrual, unchanged ownership, and reconciled owner prior reporting. This does not resolve a reduced final exclusion shared by two owners. |
| Reduced joint bond exclusion | **Interpretation requires qualified tax review.** Pub550 establishes actual income ownership; IRC135/Form8815 reduce the joint exclusion for expenses and MAGI. The inspected sources do not prescribe attribution of that reduced amount to individual educators under62(d)(2). Obtain the controlling allocation and rounding method, then implement/test it. No discretionary split is accepted. Full exclusion and sole actual income ownership are implemented in195f018. |
| In-kind QTP source ordering | **Law-supported internal work next.** Section529(c)(3)(B)(i)/(ii) excludes actual qualifying benefits and subtracts those costs before cash distributions use remaining expenses. Add actual benefit-to-expense/source linkage, date/recipient checks and conservation; preserve explicit credit/ESA elections and reject unsupported combinations. The current allocation ledger prevents reuse but does not establish the actual benefit source. |
| Prepaid/in-kind QTP educator reduction | **Interpretation requires qualified tax review.** Section529(c)(3)(B)(i) separately excludes qualifying in-kind benefits, and(ii) reduces cash QEE by those benefits. Final Schedule1line11 says QTP earnings or distributions; linked Pub529 describes earnings. The inspected sources do not expressly resolve whether an educator's prepaid reduction is the gross benefit or earnings component. Establish that treatment and cash/ESA/credit ordering before implementation. Actual medium is already collected; cash earnings treatment is implemented. |
| Broader Coverdell code6 | **Interpretation and separate source routing remain.** Section530(e) incorporates rules similar to408(e)(2)/(4), but the instructions do not explain the interaction of positive education expenses with a deemed event, partial pledge basis ordering, or later income after account termination. Qualified review must resolve each method; implementation then needs actual event/value/basis/activity facts and owner routing. The whole-account, actual-zero-expense, no-later-activity method is implemented in4421801. |

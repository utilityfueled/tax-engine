# Outside-child insurer cash and a completed other-return credit

This private package retains actual owner/business insurer cash for a qualified child outside the tax family when the other completed return has positive premium tax credit or the original outside-person advances are positive. It composes the same source contract with the existing ordinary, actual-cash, determined own-return repayment and elected-marriage methods. It does not enter a deduction, assign another taxpayer's repayment, or change a production qualification gate.

## Authority and bounded derivation

Primary sources refreshed on 2026-10-27 are [TD 9683, preamble section 6, and Revenue Procedure 2014-41, sections 3–4](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2014-33_IRB), [TD 9822 final adoption](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2017-33_IRB), [Regulation 1.162(l)-1(a)(1)–(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.162(l)-1), [Publication 974, Nonspecified Premiums](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p974), and [Form 7206 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i7206). The preamble distinguishes the payer's own credit from a child's credit. An otherwise qualified child under 27 can remain eligible for section 162(l) outside the payer's tax family, while those premiums are nonspecified for the payer. This is a source-backed composition of those rules, not a published combined numerical example or professional filing qualification.

The source attachment keeps the completed other Form 8962, exact claimant/year/policy links, original receipt/person/payer cash, actual dated reimbursements and an independently complete other-return tax-payment ledger. Actual net insurer cash is gross cash less those reimbursements. Neither the other return's credit, its advances nor its tax payments change that cash calculation. Every actual insurer payment must match its original person/payer receipt exactly once. A supplied trace is validated even in the previously supported zero-credit/zero-advance cohort. Unknown history remains distinct from an explicit empty list.

The completed other-return source is a sufficient cohort, not a universal statutory requirement or a statement that positive other-return credit disallows the parent's deduction. Own-return credits and repayments retain their existing methods and final-return checks. In the zero-own-advance method, every original policy is bound and advances attributable to this return must be zero; independently recorded outside advances may remain positive. In the actual-paid method the final return must still have zero own credit and full own reported repayment after the proposed deduction. No other-return repayment is transferred. Gross original cash plus outside advances remains bounded by its independently allocated premium pool before any reimbursement reduction.

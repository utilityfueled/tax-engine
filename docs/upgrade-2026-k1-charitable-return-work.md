# Source-bound K-1 charitable return handoff

This package connects the reviewed optional K-1 basis calculation to the canonical return. The published 190 property / 100 basis / 25 capital-loss / 80 stock-basis example now transfers 154 to the charitable candidate and 16 of capital loss, preserving separate 36 and 9 basis suspensions. It also supports current cash, qualified current stock, established 2025 prior charitable-basis releases, and an independently confirmed ordinary QBI loss source. The guided collector is a separate required follow-up; this package does not qualify unconfirmed legacy aggregate amounts or final 2026 filing.

## Sources and boundary

The source spec links [IRC 702 and 706](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t26/s702/), [704(d)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t26/s704/), [1366(d)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t26/s1366/), [170](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t26/s170/), current partner/shareholder instructions and [Form 6198 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i6198). The optional basis prerequisite retains its independently reviewed [IRS partnership FAQ example](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/new-limits-on-partners-shares-of-partnership-losses-frequently-asked-questions) and [Revenue Ruling 2008-16](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2008-11_IRB) controls.

Each original K-1 A/E/F amount binds to its original entity, taxpayer, statement period, per-gift value/basis, recipient category, stock dates and prior basis history. A private nonoperating foundation's nonqualified stock is reduced to basis before the entity basis limit, while retaining its statutory 20% category. Unknown qualification or a conflicting attached amount rejects. Current entity-year cash uses actual DAF and supporting-organization answers; neither an entity basis suspension nor a section 170 carry is mislabeled as current cash.

Section 469's charitable exclusion does not establish a section 465 exemption. This cohort requires a complete actual simplified Form 6198 source with independently known-empty prior at-risk history and capacity at least equal to every basis-allowed deduction plus the entire charitable amount, including appreciation. This sufficient, nonbinding condition makes the unresolved activity allocation immaterial to the current allowance. It is not a conclusion that charity is exempt from section 465. Positive royalty, rental, special-gain, section 179, investment-interest and other unrepresented activity deductions remain separate methods. Known positive prior noncharitable basis releases retain their independent QBI/character boundary.

[Reg. 1.702-1(a)(4)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.702-1) with section 706 and [Reg. 1.1366-1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.1366-1) support current fiscal entity-year cash inclusion. The 2026 section 170(p) application is a derived statutory reading, not a published matching worked example. Actual payment and inclusion dates remain separate. Positive 2026 prior-basis release retains a precise unresolved section 170(p)/new floor/carry contribution-year boundary: the established basis-release authorities do not expressly settle the new section 170(d)(1)(C) vintage. A known zero release preserves its actual history and suspended basis. General NOL coordination also remains explicitly rejected before any replay can discard the new source packet.

## Calculation and review output

One validated basis result supplies affected Schedule D and Schedule E amounts. The same actual charitable vector enters Schedule A, current receipt candidates and nonitemized calculations before the percentage limits, floor and final deduction selection. Actual QBI source records remain separate and cannot receive the charitable deduction. The ordinary-loss example divides 10,000 basis between 10,000 ordinary loss and 10,000 cash: 5,000 ordinary loss and 5,000 charity are allowed after basis, with separate basis and QBI balances.

A server-local opaque charitable review context retains the original input and an independently recomputed federal result. It accepts only the original source or its exact identifier-redacted copy. Changed owner/receipt/basis/risk facts, omitted packets, forged contexts and altered monetary/audit balances reject before template access. It does not reconstruct identifiers. Existing QBI contexts remain independent. Schedule A, D, 8582 and the Form 7206 income-source reconciliation use the original facts privately while rendering the redacted input. The new appendix uses ordinal references and exact amounts, distinguishes the Schedule A candidate from the selected deduction method, and keeps basis suspensions separate from charitable carryovers.

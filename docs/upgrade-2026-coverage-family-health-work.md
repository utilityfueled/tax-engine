# Actual coverage-family health sources

This package collects and derives the monthly premium split for a Marketplace plan covering both coverage-family and noncoverage-family people. It supports ordinary policies and the completed Situation 4 and Situation 3 allocation methods. It retains full original policies, independent person payments and advances, and actual benchmark sources through the guided interview, canonical calculation and private review context.

The rule is implemented for one actual specified-premium business with complete coverage sources. A supplied Part IV agreement does not establish who paid a premium. Missing membership, payment, advance or benchmark facts remain unknown. Production 2026 calculation and filing gates stay closed; isolated 2026 controls do not qualify a filing release.

## Source and calculation

Official sources refreshed October 26, 2026: [Pub974, specified premiums, Gary/Sue, Worksheets W/X and Simplified Step 3](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p974), [Form8962 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8962), and [Reg1.162(l)-1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.162(l)-1).

The original tax-family allocation precedes the health allocation. The latter uses the actual coverage-family SLCSP divided by the combined coverage-family and noncoverage-family SLCSP. The full tax-family enrollment premium remains on Form8962. Actual advances are independent source amounts and are not multiplied by this benchmark ratio. This route requires explicit zero advances for noncoverage members.

The one-business Simplified Step 3 subtracts the full corresponding credit before adding the completed nonspecified deduction. Lines 7 and 9 retain their signed intermediate values. The final transfer rounds the combined amount once. This corrects an implementation gap rather than asserting an unavailable credit-attribution rule. The established wholly covered route retains its existing cents behavior.

The source binder reconciles current year, original policy index/snapshot, recipient and every covered person, actual covered months, full receipts, independent cash/APTC shares, both benchmark references and complete member sets. It checks eligibility across all original policies and validates real payment dates. It neither invents business income for facts-only validation nor accepts a current deduction, premium fraction or credit override.

The same saved family controls are reachable in Adjustments before blocked validation and in Credits. An edit revokes the consumed nested and root confirmations. Reconfirming one record does not confirm another. Original person and policy values survive source capture, normalization and reload; no original identifiers are sent by the browser to an external service.

# Original charitable sources and NOL carry coordination

This package connects source-complete current K-1 and digital-asset gifts to the existing single-category, single-medium regular and alternative NOL comparison. Original entity basis, receipts, dates, qualified values and independent histories remain intact. The internal comparison limits only the qualified section 170 component. It never changes an entered original or accepts a calculated allowance from the user.

The actual K-1 collector and NOL collector reach the method together. The separate crypto collector is parent-owned; its `DeductionData.cryptoDonations` presence check and combined guided regression are an integration follow-through once that type is delivered. Core crypto input and independent history validation are included here.

## Sources and literal controls

- [Regulation 1.170A-10(d), including Example 3](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.170A-10) and [Form 172 instructions, line 26](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i172) establish the existing charitable/NOL carry coordination. This package changes the actual-source handoff, not that formula.
- [Section 704(d)(3)(B)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section704&num=0&edition=prelim) and [section 1366(d)(4)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section1366&num=0&edition=prelim) distinguish appreciation from the basis-limited charitable deduction. The existing original K-1 source method applies before section 170 limits.
- [Regulation 1.702-1(a)(4)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.702-1) and [1.1366-1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.1366-1) retain the included entity-year contribution and character. A current included fiscal-year gift is not rejected merely because the actual payment occurred in the preceding calendar year.
- [Notice 2014-21](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-14-21.pdf), section 170(e), and the existing qualified digital-asset source method preserve actual holding dates, per-gift basis, recipient classification and substantiation.

The new numerical controls are derived source examples, not published matching IRS examples. With income 100,000 and regular NOL 100,000, a qualified C source 60,000 yields a 56,000 current NOL deduction, 44,000 AGI, 13,200 current charity, and 33,360 succeeding regular charitable carry. Independent known-zero ATNOL history leaves 46,800 alternative charitable carry. A private nonoperating crypto gift with 60,000 FMV and 20,000 basis instead supplies D 20,000 and yields 2,560 regular charitable carry. K-1 FMV 80,000, allocated basis 40,000 and available owner basis 20,000 supplies C 60,000 while preserving a separate 20,000 basis suspension. The positive-ATNOL control retains the actual 61,110 current claim and distinct 30,090 alternative charitable carry.

## Reproduced failures and corrections

The spec preceded five failing core cases: blanket original-source rejection and an unconfirmed small gift bypass. Separate red tests then reproduced stale crypto source/output acceptance and missing independent guided history acceptance. The implementation validates original sources before checking whether a charitable excess remains, binds all original input and output through a private pre-redaction context, and keeps the actual independent history requirement visible in guided validation.

The production browser exposed a saved-section callback defect. Confirming NOL history on a source-only adjustment section inserted six absent blank legacy fields and revoked linked K-1/history confirmations. A permanent DOM regression reproduced that exact shape; the NOL callback now changes only its saved source field. Other adjustment callbacks are unchanged. The original browser red log and fictional saved state are retained. Later browser attempts corrected test-only navigation (use Continue from an earlier section) and existing comma-formatted display expectations; no further product change or assertion removal occurred.

One adjacent test expected the old blanket K-1/NOL rejection. Its last assertion now expects the required independent history, while the unsupported legacy-source assertion remains unchanged. An initial artifact reader expected a regular carry phrase used only when a regular NOL comparison exists; the independent-AMT case correctly uses `Current regular charitable carry`. The reader expectation was corrected, with product and artifact bytes unchanged. The saved workspace test respects the existing policy that explicit business EINs remain on basis references; taxpayer identifiers and private source references are omitted.

## Finite remaining scope

This current-original source method is bounded to one actual qualified category/medium, independent known-empty prior charitable histories and the established original basis/at-risk cohort. Positive K-1 basis releases, current-origin Form 172/COD, positive prior charitable histories and mixed categories are not silently reclassified.

Remaining implementable work is the ordinary current-origin charity handoff and source-derived standard/nonitemized NOL coordination. Remaining interpretation questions are exact allocation of aggregate NOL benefit among multiple categories or original vintages, the 2026 within-category floor attribution across current/prior and basis-release origins, and partial same-category B cash/property attribution. Category/FIFO ordering alone does not establish those allocations. They remain internal source-method questions, distinct from credentials, human review and form/year qualification.

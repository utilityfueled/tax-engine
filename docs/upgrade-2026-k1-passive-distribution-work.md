# Single passive activity cash-distribution source method

This package extends the existing original cash-distribution calculation to a continuing single ordinary passive business. The taxpayer must supply a complete entity activity and valuation report, actual participation and property character, original cash/holding/exclusion records, a reconciled actual Form6198 gain item and the existing complete basis, prior469 and QBI histories. No gain or allocation percentage is entered. Original source records remain encrypted/full fidelity; render copies use the existing private capital and charitable contexts.

## Source derivation

Refreshed 2026-10-26: [Pub925](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p925) explicitly includes excess cash distributions in the partners/shareholders partial-disposition rule. [Reg1.469-2T(e)(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.469-2T) allocates by appreciated activities or the FMV fallback. A complete actual single activity gives one allocation under either branch. Actual holder-allocable underlying asset shares and743(b) adjustments remain distinct; the source does not supply a tax allocation override. Calendar entity-year and original distribution timing restrictions remain unchanged.

The entity's actual beginning-year/distribution-date election controls unless original event aggregates trigger the regulation's greater-than10% exception, when the preceding day applies. Each row is one complete actual activity aggregate. Duplicate actual names cannot disguise two6% dispositions as separate activities. Business cash means actual noninvestment U.S. cash with equal FMV/basis; interest-bearing reserves are portfolio sources, as illustrated by the regulation's portfolio-income example. No material participation, rental, oil/gas, licensing, former nonpassive property, special capital or recapture character is assumed.

[Form8582 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8582) govern the ordinary-loss use and reporting. The gain supplies activity income but remains on8949/D. Actual allowed losses remain ordinary ScheduleE amounts; continuing ownership never creates a full469(g) release. Original net capital gain is excluded from QBI under199A(c)(3)(B). [Form8960 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8960) and [Pub596](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p596) retain independent capital and ordinary passive components. Form8582 rounds combined source income at its existing line; the ordinary transfer retains its original reported component so crossing a combined rounding threshold does not create ordinary income.

## Finite remaining method boundaries

The existing investment-only source method remains supported. This package's ordinary passive route requires original nonbinding465 capacity, no prior-basis or prior-at-risk releases, no positive Scorp-debt QBI source method, a complete single continuing non-PTP activity, and no Form461 business-capital allocation. Multi-activity/nonpassive lookthrough, fiscal/interim partnership basis, property/mixed distributions,751/special holding, E&P/redemption, and binding469/465 character across other source classes remain distinct internal methods. The original2026 calculation/export and annual NOL/charity boundaries stay closed.

The actual valuation/participation collector is the next coherent follow-through and will reuse the existing distribution attachment, original QBI activity link, confirmation invalidation and unknown values. It will not collect a computed gain or passive allocation. This core alone does not claim that guide reachability or the broader distribution queue is complete.

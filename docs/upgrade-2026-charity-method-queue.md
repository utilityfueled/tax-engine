## Four precise source interpretation questions

These are bounded research findings, not claims that no controlling authority exists. A supported rule and actual source contract must precede spec-first implementation. A confirmation checkbox cannot supply the rule.

1. **Floor reaching a prior contribution within a category.** Category order is prescribed, but a positive hit on prior treated-paid contributions needs original-year attribution. This includes current/prior mixtures, pre-2026 balances and the distinct contribution-year treatment of a released entity-basis deduction. Current-only hits and zero floor are delivered. No matching vintage-allocation example was established in the reviewed [IRC 170(b)(1)(I), (d)(1)(C)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t26/s170/) and [Reg. 1.170A-10](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.170A-10) sources.
2. **Positive NOL benefit with multiple original excesses.** Aggregate absorption does not establish how its reduction is split among original categories/vintages/media. The explicit public-before-other rule does not settle every split within those groups. Zero benefit and one positive excess origin are delivered. Mixed section 170(p) recipient comparisons also reject when feasible retained sources yield different reported deductions. [Reg. 1.170A-10(d)(1)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.170A-10) and [Form 172 line 26](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i172) support the aggregate comparison, not invented marginal replay order.
3. **Partial B allowance or floor between cash and property.** B aggregates these media. No prescribed cash-first, pro-rata or discretionary allocation was established by the bounded primary-source review. Single-medium and exact zero/full cases are delivered. See [IRC 170(b)(1)(B), (I)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t26/s170/), [Publication 526](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p526), and [Reg. 1.170A-8](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.170A-8).
4. **Prior C treated-paid use under the amended B offset.** The initial treated-paid amount and final deductible amount are separate stages. Where the amended B reservation makes them differ, neither can silently become carry consumption. Current-C reservation and reconciling prior-C combinations are delivered. The unresolved transition is the conflicting prior-C/B cohort. See [IRC 170(b)(1)(B), (C)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t26/s170/), [Reg. 1.170A-10(c)(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.170A-10), and Publication 526's carryover example.

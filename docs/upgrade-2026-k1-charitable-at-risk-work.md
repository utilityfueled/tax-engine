# Actual charitable activity deductions under section 465

This package calculates a binding at-risk limitation from actual activity-assignment sources after the existing K-1 basis calculation. It retains income, allocates the disallowed loss among the remaining deduction characters, and sends only the permitted charitable amount to section 170. Basis suspensions, at-risk suspensions and five-year charitable carryovers remain separate. No calculated allowance is collected.

## Authority and source contract

[Reg. 1.469-2T(d)(6)(iii)–(iv)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.469-2T) and [Publication 925, proration of deductions disallowed under the at-risk limitation](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p925) allocate each deduction's disallowed portion as its amount times the disallowed activity loss divided by the sum of activity deductions. Basis-disallowed amounts are removed first. [Form 6198 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i6198) retain reportable income and gains and allocate only the disallowed loss. [Section 465(d)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t26/s465/) defines the activity loss. These official sources were refreshed October 26, 2026.

The section 469 charitable exclusion does not establish a section 465 exemption. This method requires an additional original attachment identifying every charitable source exactly once and confirming that all reconciled items belong to the same activity. All eight actual simplified Form 6198 monetary inputs, its activity identity, source year and reference, and an independently confirmed empty prior at-risk ledger remain required. Line 4 must contain the full charitable value after basis, including the appreciation exception. Original ordinary, short-term and long-term amounts, other income and any already-supported distribution gain reconcile to the completed Part I sources. A computed at-risk override cannot replace them.

The existing nonbinding method remains available without a new assignment: actual capacity must cover every basis-allowed deduction and the full charitable value. This package does not silently choose activity membership for that method. It also does not assert that the regulation's passive-coordination context exempts other covered activities from the source-specific allocation: the denominator and income-retention rule are corroborated by the general IRS at-risk instructions.

## Actual intake and review output

Adding it creates no affirmative answer, source ID, amount or calculated allowance. Existing original K-1, basis, risk and QBI sources remain the facts. An original with separate nonzero capital or investment amounts must preserve an explicit false answer to the old noncharitable-zero statement and supply the matching actual original and assignment. A legacy true answer cannot authorize those amounts. Editing sources revokes dependent confirmation and blocks preview/export until complete again.

Retained original-source audit or at-risk carry evidence requires the original packet even if a caller removes the entire attachment.

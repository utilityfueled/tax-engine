## Authority and determined payment scope

The regulation limits specified premiums by attributable credit and by cash plus attributable advance repayment. Pub502 distinguishes actual repayment from advances that remain unpaid. Pub974's optional-method permission does not, by itself, prove a payment allocation.

This is a derived single eligible payment-pool case, not a published numerical example. One actual owner/business plan must account for all policy advances on this return. Every source cost is eligible under the same health rules and own-business income limit. Each month's original coverage-person cash plus advances must equal the independently derived specified premium; the noncoverage-person component must separately equal the nonspecified premium. Original tax-family/PartIV allocations and actual payer shares must reconcile first. A benchmark ratio is not substituted for a payment share.

At actual final credit zero, let specified and nonspecified costs be S and N, their actual advances As and An, and their attributable repayments Rs and Rn. Within original expense bounds, the paid amounts add to `(S - As + Rs) + (N - An + Rn) = actual cash + actual repayment`. Both components have the same qualification and business limit, so this sum does not require choosing an ownership or tax allocation. Repayment cannot create cost above the original `S + N`. The resulting common paid amount is `min(original eligible cost, actual cash + reported repayment)`, followed by the actual own-business income limit. This derivation is admitted only with the component-source and common-qualification conditions above; it is not a generic aggregate-bound formula for positive credit, different eligible people or competing business pools.

The engine derives the original annual/monthly advance-reporting convention from the actual completed Form8962. It calculates the complete return again using the derived paid cost, including other income-sensitive claims, and requires actual final credit zero and actual repayment equal to reported original advances. Exact source advances remain separately retained. An unpaid source-cent remainder is never described as repaid; a reported round-up cannot create an expense above original premium cost. There is no entered current repayment, credit, MAGI or deduction.

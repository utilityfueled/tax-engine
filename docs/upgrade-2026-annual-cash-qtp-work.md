# Annual ordinary cash QTP source method

This private package applies the annual cash fraction to complete ordinary same-year, same-beneficiary and same-recipient postsecondary QTP originals without an ESA. It preserves each actual Box 1, Box 2, Box 3, receipt and original allocation. Period caps, actual prior cap use, assistance, credit and determined prepaid costs are resolved first; the remaining annual cash expense is compared with all actual cash distributions, including an original with no receipt allocation. Calculated account shares are not taxpayer elections or source edits.

Authority refreshed October 26, 2026: IRC 529(c)(3)(B)(ii), (v), (vi) and (e)(3)(B), current IRS Publication 970 chapter 7, current Instructions for Form 1099-Q, and Notice 2016-13. The latter removes account aggregation for determining distributed earnings; it does not authorize a new allocation election. Each actual reported Box 2 is retained. Ratios and products use a source-sized local Decimal context, and recipient and return line totals add exact source contributions before whole-dollar reporting. Raw and exact calculated values remain separate in the audit. The literal cases are independently derived controls, not published IRS examples.

The period helper retains actual per-original receipt membership. When several cash originals share a reduced residual, it retains that cash capacity once for the annual fraction instead of inventing a source allocation. Existing unique prepaid and actual ESA choices remain separate. The earlier inactive-source and separate-period tests retain their source-map checks; only the old cash tax expectations change to the annual rule. Prepaid assertions are unchanged. The private education context now also binds a complete annual cash pool before identifier redaction, including an ordinary tuition group without period records.

Finite boundaries remain explicit: actual transfers, corrections, special expense categories, mixed recipients, missing originals, known outside-plan distributions lacking their original gross/earnings, and competing prepaid originals under an unresolved binding cap cannot become this ordinary no-ESA pool. ESA coordination is not expanded. No source field stores a calculated exclusion, and no readiness, tax-year, e-file, coverage or independent-review gate changes.

# Explicit nonitemized prior entity property

This private package admits actual property released from an existing entity
basis or section 465 limitation only when the 2026 taxpayer explicitly selects
the standard deduction or the supported nonitemized choice. It uses the existing
percentage-use ledger. Property within that capacity is not preserved merely
because no itemized deduction is claimed. Original receipt, basis and risk years
and unreleased balances remain separate from new section 170 percentage excess.

The original source packet still supplies every amount and confirmation. No
current deduction, release, allocation or carry can be entered as a replacement.
Automatic and itemized choices retain the unresolved prior-gift floor-year
boundary. Prior cash retains its separate section 170(p) timing boundary.

## Authority and derived controls

Primary sources refreshed October 28, 2026:

- [Reg. 1.170A-10(a)(2), (b)(2), (c)(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.170A-10)
  separates percentage excess from the choice to itemize and treats available
  prior section 170 carry as paid within annual capacity.
- [IRC 704(d)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-124/t26/s704/),
  [Reg. 1.704-1(d)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.704-1),
  [Reg. 1.1366-2(a)(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.1366-2)
  and [IRC 465(a)(2)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-121/t26/s465/)
  supply the existing release and retained-character methods.
- [PL 119-21, section 70424](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ21/html/PLAW-119publ21.htm)
  and [IRS Topic 506](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc506) limit the new
  nonitemizer deduction to qualifying cash; released property is not cash.

These combined controls are independently derived, not published IRS examples.
At a $100,000 contribution base, original C property released at $20,000, $30,000
or $40,000 produces no selected property deduction and new percentage carry of
$0, $0 or $10,000. Separate original basis or risk balances of $60,000 leave
$40,000, $30,000 or $20,000 suspended in their original ledgers. D property uses
its existing 20% capacity. Current qualifying cash remains separately eligible.

The combined-source control releases $40,000 of original 2018 basis beside an
independent $60,000 original 2024 risk balance. Actual risk capacity of $50,000
allows $20,000 and $30,000 respectively. The $20,000 basis suspension, $20,000
new risk suspension, $30,000 old risk balance and $20,000 section 170 excess
remain distinct. Previously allowed appreciation is never repeated.

Existing nonitemized NOL comparison also composes with these sources. Wages of
$100,000, the actual $16,100 standard deduction and an actual $100,000 old
post-2017 NOL produce a $67,120 current NOL deduction, $32,880 AGI and $9,864 C
capacity. Releases of $5,000, $9,864 and $20,000 produce percentage excess of
$0, $0 and $10,136, with zero charitable comparison benefit. Independent AMT
histories remain required. This changes no NOL monetary formula or priority.

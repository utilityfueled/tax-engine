The prepared prepaid-tuition, academic-period room-and-board and annual-cash education contexts previously bound their education subresult and taxpayer, but whole-return PDF consumers could receive unrelated changed original income or changed federal results. The source-complete prepaid control has $200 taxable earnings and $50,200 AGI; changing only AGI to $50,201 reached template access in both `fillReturn` and direct `fillForm5329`.

The private context now retains complete original-input and federal-result digests for these nonannual-interest cohorts. Both existing whole-return consumers already supply the complete result. They reject stale sources, changed federal values/audits and missing full results before template/output access. Only separately attached `stateReturns` is excluded from the new federal-result digest; state-owned validation remains separate. The existing annual-interest full-result/deduction checks and low-level education-only reference API remain unchanged. No tax formula, source qualification, year gate, PDF layout or renderer is changed.

The literal controls follow [IRS Publication 970 (2025), chapter 7, Taxable Earnings](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p970), retrieved November 6, 2026: reduce qualified expenses by tax-free educational assistance, multiply earnings by the adjusted-expense/distribution ratio, then subtract excluded earnings. The existing complete prepaid original gives $600 excluded and $200 taxable; the existing period and annual-cash originals give $800 and $3,500 taxable. With their unchanged $50,000 wages and no adjustments, AGIs are $50,200, $50,800 and $53,500. An independent Decimal/Fraction oracle was saved before product execution.

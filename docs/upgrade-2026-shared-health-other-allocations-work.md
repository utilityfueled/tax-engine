# Shared-health default and no-advance sources

This separate extension builds on the delivered shared-health agreement package, commit `0b65e34fde7cf48e6b2b5142f5e372c92f376beb`. It uses the existing actual enrollment-default and no-advance benchmark allocators before the same independent person/payment binder. No missing cash payment, advance, benchmark or agreement is inferred.

Sources refreshed October 26, 2026: [Pub974, Specified Premiums](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p974), [final i8962 Situations3/4 and Gary/Jim example](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8962). Pub974 directs tax-family policy allocation first, without an agreement-only restriction. Situation4's actual enrollment default and Situation3's exact benchmark ratio already have independently reviewed source calculators. Pub974 W18 bypasses WorksheetX when attributable advances are zero; the existing health method retains that distinction.

The derived statutory controls preserve the distinction between source amounts, exact calculation and printed allocation:

| Actual source method | Monthly original and allocated sources | Canonical result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two-person enrollment default | Full premium $1,000 and APTC $600; allocated premium $500 and APTC $300; separately sourced owner cash $200 and other-family cash $200 | Health deduction $2,340; AGI $44,127; PTC $3,480; repayment $120 |
| No advance credit | Full premium $1,500; actual family benchmarks $1,000/$500; exact allocated premium $1,000; original and individual APTC zero; owner cash $1,000 and other-family cash $500 | Health deduction $1,080; AGI $45,387; PTC $9,264; repayment zero |

The no-advance method prints `.67` in Part IV but calculates with the exact two-thirds ratio. Pub974's first replay has a $12,000 maximum, $34,467 AGI, 228% of the poverty line and .0312 applicable figure: $90 monthly contribution gives $10,920 credit and a $1,080 health deduction. The final replay has $45,387 AGI, 301% and .0603: $228 monthly contribution gives $9,264 credit. A separate $1,000,000 business-income control gives the full $12,000 health deduction and zero PTC. These are source-derived controls, not published IRS or human-qualified oracles.

A separate spec-linked control splits the same original premium into two $750 policies covering the same people. Complete monthly coverage-family groups retain the $1,000/$500 benchmark once, and the actual combined receipt still conserves $1,500. Reordering linked policies leaves the result unchanged; omitting the complete overlapping coverage-family evidence rejects.

Spec and tests preceded the product change. Both new tests reproduced the agreement-only guard in `.local/upgrade-evidence/shared-health-other-allocations-red.log`. The sole source-calculation change admits the two methods only after the existing pure allocator validates their complete original sources. Payment, identity, family, owner, monthly completeness and conservation checks stay in force.

Canonical, guide and private-output checks use the existing saved source model. The production desktop default and mobile no-advance flows restore source records after reload, revoke confirmations on edits, and preserve a blank paid share as unknown. The saved original $1,500 is never replaced with its allocated $1,000. Physical Form8962 prints the allocation and credit while the health review appendix retains the original, each payer's amount, the specified premium and the final deduction. Stale benchmark context is rejected before PDF I/O.

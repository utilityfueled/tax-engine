Users enter the original valuation date, entity date selection, complete annual activity totals, actual asset identities, holder shares of fair market value and adjusted asset basis, separate owner basis adjustments and participation facts. The same original K-1 remains linked to its actual basis, Form6198, prior469 and QBI histories. No gain, allocation ratio or current loss allowance is entered.

The source contract and authority remain in `spec/federal/k1-passive-distribution-gain.yaml`; this package has no new tax formula or metadata version. `spec/federal/guided-k1-passive-distribution.yaml` was written before its failing source and interaction cases.

The adapter now normalizes only supplied original spelling and recognizes the existing validated passive-distribution result. It preserves and rejects a supplied incompatible classification. Ordinary K-1 routes retain their existing classification requirement. Known original owner-status answers are forwarded to the same source validators instead of omitted in the linked path.

Source lists start unknown; new rows contain no invented IDs, amounts, dates or confirmations. Exact decimal strings, negative owner adjustments, actual zero, false and unknown remain distinct. Substantive edits revoke nested confirmations, including the activity report and linked loss packet. Unsupported original activity character remains visible and saved. The panel labels the asset basis as adjusted basis before the separate owner adjustment.

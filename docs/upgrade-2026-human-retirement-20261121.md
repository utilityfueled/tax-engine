# Independent review of IRA distributions and Form 5329

The assignments are open. Give a qualified reviewer the fictional facts and a blank worksheet first. Ask for the authority and form revisions, the line-by-line calculation, required original records, and a dated disposition before sharing the software results. Record the applicable 2026 form status separately.

## First-home purchase paid from traditional IRA proceeds

An unmarried taxpayer born July 15, 1980 receives one $7,000 traditional IRA payment on September 15, 2025. Form 1099-R shows $7,000 in both Boxes 1 and 2a, an unchecked taxable-amount-undetermined box, zero in Box 5, and Code 1 with the IRA checkbox marked. The taxpayer has zero IRA basis. The payment is from the taxpayer’s own account and is neither rolled over nor returned. A binding contract for the taxpayer’s own main home is signed October 20, 2025. The taxpayer completes the purchase and pays $6,500 of the purchase price from that distribution on January 13, 2026. The taxpayer held no ownership interest in a main home during the preceding two years and has no former section 1034 suspension. The taxpayer previously used $4,000 of qualifying first-home distributions.

Determine the IRA gross and taxable income, the 120-day deadline, the remaining lifetime amount and Form 5329 Part I lines 1 through 4. Identify the Schedule 2 transfer and whether a Form 5329 should accompany a fully excepted Code 1 distribution. Repeat with January 14 as the payment date. For a separate fully excepted control, use the original January 13 date, a $7,000 purchase-price payment from the same proceeds and $3,000 of prior lifetime use. Explain how to handle unknown prior usage, a changed statement account and an additional unresolved retirement exception. [26 U.S.C. § 72(t)(8)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapB-partII-sec72.htm) · [2025 Publication 590-B](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/p590b--2025.pdf#page=27) · [2025 Form 5329 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i5329--2025.pdf#page=5).

## Receipt on either side of age 59½

A single taxpayer born July 15, 1965 took one $6,000 distribution from their own traditional IRA in 2025. Box 1 and Box 2a both show $6,000. The statement has Code 1, the IRA checkbox is marked, the taxable-amount-undetermined box is unchecked and Box 5 is zero. The taxpayer has zero basis and confirms that the full amount arrived in one payment. Compare January 14 and January 15, 2025 as alternate receipt dates. For a separate 2026 control, use a taxpayer born July 15, 1966 and compare January 14 and January 15, 2026 under the same statement facts.

Determine the applicable age on the actual receipt date, the Part I exception code and lines 1 through 4, and the effect of a missing receipt date. Explain what an original Box 5 amount of $300 would require the reviewer to reconcile with a general representation of zero IRA basis. State the Form 5329 attachment requirement when the payer’s Code 1 conflicts with the actual age.

# Actual Marketplace cash independent of policy allocations

## Scope and source derivation

This private package implements a derived sufficient method for otherwise qualified owner/business cash that differs from the tax-family policy allocation. It adds no input tax amount, payment percentage, election or confirmation. Original policies, Part IV allocation, actual monthly family eligibility/SLCSP sources and actual receipts remain independent. The bounded software/source method and saved artifacts received independent agent review below; human tax/form qualification remains separate.

Primary sources refreshed on 2026-10-26:

- [26 CFR 1.162(l)-1(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.162(l)-1) limits the specified deduction by the premium less attributable credit and by the premium less advances plus attributable repayment.
- [Publication 974, Specified Premiums](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p974) orders actual Part IV allocation before the coverage-family SLCSP split and keeps nonspecified premiums separate. Its Gary/Sue example establishes the gross 15,000 to 10,000/5,000 split; the present unequal-payment cases are derived controls, not IRS examples.
- [Rev. Proc. 2014-41, sections 3-4](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2014-33_IRB) makes its calculation methods optional while requiring any other method to satisfy applicable law, and treats otherwise deductible nonspecified premiums separately. This is the alternative-method authority; current Publication 974 has no “Other Methods” heading.
- [Form 7206 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i7206) require actual qualifying payments, person/payer/plan eligibility, employer-month conditions and the owner's actual business limit.
- [Form 8962 instructions, line 26](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8962) leave lines 27–29 blank when lines 24 and 25 are equal. A blank physical repayment line is separate from the calculated zero repayment.

Let F be the independently allocated tax-family premium, S its source-derived coverage-family portion and N=F-S. Actual own-person cash is recorded separately as Ps and Pn. This bounded method requires Ps<=S and Pn<=N for every plan/month, explicit zero advances on all original statements, and ordinary health eligibility before deduction. With completed C=A=R=0, both specified regulatory limits equal S; no positive credit or repayment must be attributed. The claimed ordinary amount is the actual eligible Ps+Pn, limited by that owner's business, not F. Nonspecified cash is bounded separately; unused N cannot cure excess Ps. Exact source amounts are retained until the existing separate-business reporting rules apply.

The complete canonical return is calculated using the proposed ordinary cash deduction, including dependent-income effects and all other claims. The method accepts only verified final zero credit, zero net credit, zero advances and zero repayment. A source-only guide result cannot establish those tax facts. Equal actual cash plus advances and allocated premium retain the previous method.

- Coverage-person cash 833.34 exceeds the exact specified component even if total cash fits F. Missing advances, duplicate people and unreconciled receipts reject.

July–December coverage retains only 2,700 paid cash. Disqualifying actual employer coverage conditions produce zero health deduction.

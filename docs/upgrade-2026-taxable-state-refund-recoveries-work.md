The historical amount receives its own tax-benefit worksheet; current payments of 1,000 become a deduction of 900. Box 3 identifies the state-return year and never substitutes for the payment dates.

The finite method covers 2025/2026 receipts and ordinary same-owner single/MFJ 2024/2025 historical deductions. It binds original 1099-G statements, current taxpayers, complete dated payment basis and exact current estimated/extension-payment links. Each earlier year receives one grouped worksheet, preserving shared deduction capacity across multiple refunds. Current allocations reduce SALT once and do not enter Schedule 1 income. Unknown special tax, credits, carryovers, historical ownership or unsupported years remain denied. Legacy scalar worksheets and historical guards stay intact.

Official sources were retrieved again on 2026-10-26:

- [IRC 32(k)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-124/t26/s32/) supplies the two- and ten-year restrictions.
- [26 CFR 1.32-3](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.32-3) separates actual deficiency assessment, a properly completed recertification, later allowance and omission of a required form. Paragraph (e) expressly applies the unresolved pre-marriage history of either spouse to a joint return. The display is current through 2026-10-09.
- [Final 2025 Publication 596, chapter 5](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p596) supplies the five timing examples and the actual child-only exception.
- [December 2025 Form 8862 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8862) supply the later-allowance and no-qualifying-child exceptions, actual form requirements and separate appeal procedure.
- [IRS CP79A guidance](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/understanding-your-cp79a-notice) identifies the next permitted year shown in the actual notice.

The resolver separately binds the current tax year, actual filer identity, current claim status, original return filings, notice dates, deficiency assessments, final conduct determinations, intervening original returns and actual subsequent allowances. Preparing a return uses an explicit preparation-as-of date and history reviewed through that date, without inventing a filing. An already-filed claim uses its actual filing date. Null assessment/finality means an explicitly known event has not occurred; omission means unknown. Each joint filer supplies independent records.

Published controls retain the original outcomes: a 2024 deficiency assessed in December 2025 requires recertification on the 2025 childful claim; a February 2026 notice before an unexpired assessment period does not yet require it for that filing. The published reckless-disregard examples bar 2025–2026 or 2026–2027 depending on the actual intervening filing. The published fraud example bars 2025–2034 and requires recertification afterward. Specific days within the published months are fictional test-source dates, not published daily oracles. Testing the pure duration through 2035 does not enable a 2035 return.

The ordinary chronology derivation is deliberately bounded to the published timing pattern. An actual notice's next permitted year must reconcile with that pattern when represented. Other late or out-of-order histories need the actual controlling notice; contested determinations remain a separate method. Neither the denied tax year alone nor a missing later allowance establishes a ban's starting year. A source-derived child-only exception uses actual qualifying-child presence, independently of the SSN-based amount count.

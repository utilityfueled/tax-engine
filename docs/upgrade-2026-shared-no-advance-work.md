# Shared policy with no advance credit

This package implements the ordinary two-family Situation 3 method from actual original statements and independently obtained coverage-family benchmarks. Original zero, a known printed SLCSP blank, and an unknown amount remain different facts. No agreement percentage or original statement benchmark substitutes for the actual family benchmarks.

Official sources refreshed October 26, 2026: [final 2025 Form 8962 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8962), Situation 3 and Part IV columns (e)–(g), and [Reg. 1.36B-3(h)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.36B-3). Gary and Jim's published example allocates original premiums of $15,000 using $12,000/$6,000 applicable benchmarks: $10,000/$5,000. Part IV prints .67/.33. The calculation multiplies the original premium by the actual benchmark numerator before division and rounds only the printed fraction to two places. It uses each return's full applicable benchmark and leaves Part IV (f)/(g) blank. Monthly source ratios remain in the audit and review statement even when printed fractions match.

Every original month requires actual zero advances and both benchmark amounts, source references, year and covered-person identities. The initial method requires all original people to belong to the two established coverage families, positive benchmarks and nonoverlapping policy months. Unknown coverage, another allocation situation, shared/unshared overlap, marriage or health coordination retains its separate source method. No amount is supplied to make an incomplete source usable. Full 2026 and filing qualification gates remain closed.

The guide provides an explicit original printed-blank answer, copies actual covered-person/month groups, and leaves benchmark amounts and references blank. It reuses the canonical source resolver, derives household amounts once and retains separate eligibility answers. Source changes revoke outer, allocation and benchmark confirmations. Original-source review tokens bind new identities, references and exact amounts before render-only redaction; the reference distinguishes original printed blank from the return's actual benchmark.
